01

Shelf

Personal project · 2026 · Autodesk Fusion · Leimholz (pine)

A four-shelf timber unit, 648 × 450 × 1000 mm, designed parametrically in Autodesk Fusion and then built in my garage in one day from a two-length cut list (table saw, grinder, basic tools). No back panel: both sides stay open, and 48 countersunk screws (three per joint) take the sideways load instead. Built, it doesn’t wobble under load.

02

Retractable wheel system

mu-zero HYPERLOOP · Structure & Packaging · 2025–26 · Autodesk Fusion · FEM

A two-position wheel mount for the pod with no actuator. The pod runs on wheels until takeoff and then has to get them out of the way. Instead of a motor and control loop, one 14 mm spacer plate is bolted either above or below the pod skin: above, the wheel sits high; below, it drops 14 mm. Both settings use the same four M8 bolts at the same 33 mm grip length, so one torque spec covers both.

04

Gearbox

Personal project after Maschinenkonstruktionslehre A (MKL A), KIT · Autodesk Fusion

A gearbox shaft assembly, shown with the housing cut open. A steel shaft with a keyway carries the gear and runs in two bearings of different sizes. A radial shaft seal (RWDR) closes the housing; a circlip, a spacer and a cap complete the assembly. Envelope about 200 × 325 × 220 mm.
